BrandPulse 3D — v2.0 Upgrade Patch
==================================
No new pip or npm packages are required!

HOW TO APPLY THIS PATCH:
1. Extract the contents of brandpulse-3d-v2-patch.zip directly into your existing
   brandpulse-3d/ root folder and choose "Replace / Merge" when prompted.
   (Your .venv and node_modules will NOT be touched!)

2. Restart your FastAPI backend (if running) so the new domain-aware competitor engine
   and auto-seeded competitor records load into brandpulse.db:
      cd backend
      uvicorn app.main:app --reload --port 8000

3. Refresh your frontend at http://localhost:5173 (Vite hot-reloads automatically).
